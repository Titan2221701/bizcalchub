Preparing a helpful request

  • Include the page URL, calculator name, browser, and the language you are using.
  • Describe the expected result and what happened. Use made-up amounts to demonstrate an issue rather than sharing confidential business figures.
  • For accessibility feedback, explain the control or text that was difficult to use and any assistive technology involved.
  • Do not send passwords, payment details, identity documents, or personal financial records.
