Last updated: 2026-10-05

Using the tools

You may use the free calculators to explore pricing, profitability, advertising performance, investment returns, and break-even scenarios. Calculators run in your browser and require JavaScript. They do not execute transactions, prepare official filings, or provide a professional assessment of your business.

Results depend on the figures and cost scope you enter. Use the same currency and time period, include relevant expenses, and read the assumptions shown with each tool. The platform does not convert exchange rates, predict future sales, or automatically include omitted costs.

Displayed rounding can differ from unrounded values used in further analysis. Undefined results are labeled N/A; they are not zero.

Examples are illustrative and are not statements about actual companies or guaranteed returns. ROI is not annualized; ROAS does not establish net profit; break-even analysis assumes consistent unit economics. Review each guide for its particular limitations.
