Privacy Policy

This policy explains how BizCalcHub handles information when you browse the website or use its business planning tools. The website is available in English, German, and Spanish.

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Scope and current service

Advertising and analytics are not active in this version. The sections below explain how these services may work if enabled; they do not mean tracking is currently installed.

Calculator inputs

Calculator amounts are processed locally in your browser. The current calculator code does not transmit those amounts to a server, save them in a database, or retain them in local or session storage. Reset clears the form. Reopening a page does not restore your previous entries.

Hosting and technical information

Visiting a page sends a normal website request to the hosting provider. Technical logs may include an IP address, requested URL, timestamp, browser details, and error information for delivery, security, and troubleshooting. This is separate from calculator input processing.

The hosting provider, legal operator, and applicable retention periods still need to be confirmed before public launch.

Cookies and similar technologies

The current site code does not set cookies or save calculator entries in browser storage. Your browser and hosting environment may have their own behavior; this statement describes the website code, not every service used to reach it.

Cookies may be used for website functionality, analytics, and advertising purposes when the relevant services are enabled. Functional cookies can support preferences and basic features; analytics cookies can measure website use; advertising cookies can help deliver, measure, or personalize ads. Providers may also use web beacons, local storage, IP addresses, device identifiers, or similar technologies. The actual providers and controls will be disclosed before activation, and consent will be requested where required.

Google AdSense and third-party advertising

This website uses Google AdSense to display advertisements when advertising is enabled. Google and third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads. Google and participating advertising partners may collect or receive information such as IP addresses, device or browser identifiers, page visits, and ad interactions. Third parties may place and read cookies or use web beacons as a result of ad serving.

Google may use advertising cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites. Personalized advertising is subject to applicable consent requirements and your choices. Other advertising vendors may also use cookies for personalized advertising; their own privacy notices and opt-out controls apply.

How Google uses data on partner sites

Analytics

No analytics service is installed in the current version. If analytics is introduced, it may measure page views, device and browser information, approximate location, and site interactions to help improve usability. Its provider, settings, collection, retention, and consent requirements will be disclosed before activation.

Calculator input values will not be intentionally included in analytics events or advertising targeting. Avoid including confidential amounts or personal data in URLs or support requests.

Your choices and consent

You can review cookie settings in your browser. If services requiring consent are introduced, a suitable choice mechanism and a way to revisit preferences will be provided before those services are enabled. A consent management platform is not currently installed.

Personalized Google advertising for users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland requires a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. This must be configured and tested before that advertising is served.

Users may manage ad personalization settings and opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.
