How advertising relates to this platform

This disclosure explains our approach to advertising and the distinction between paid placements and business calculator content.

Last updated: 2026-10-05

Current advertising status

The site currently contains labeled advertising placeholders only. Google AdSense and other live advertising services are not installed, and no advertiser is being promoted through those placeholders.

Google AdSense and advertising partners

If advertising is enabled, Google AdSense or other third-party partners may supply ads. Advertisers are responsible for their own products, offers, landing pages, and claims. Read the advertiser's terms before making a purchase or providing information.

Advertising may be selected using context, device information, or preferences where permitted. An ad's presence does not mean that we have verified its claims or that the product is suitable for your business.

Funding and editorial independence

Advertising, if introduced, may help cover hosting and maintenance costs. Payment for an ad does not change our calculator formulas or constitute a recommendation. Examples and explanatory guides are intended to help users understand business calculations, independently of advertising.

Placement standards

Advertising must be labeled and kept separate from calculator inputs, results, navigation, and action buttons. Ads must not imitate a Calculate or Reset control, cover the tool, or require a click to access results. We do not ask users to click ads to support the website.

Privacy and advertising choices

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies when services are activated. Our Privacy Policy explains the planned disclosures and applicable consent setup. No advertising consent controls are active yet because no ad service is installed.
